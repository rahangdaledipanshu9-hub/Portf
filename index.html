01Profile

About me

I'm an entry-level cybersecurity professional based in Nagpur, India, with internship experience across three sides of the field: cybercrime investigation, web application penetration testing, and vulnerability assessment.

At the Cyber Cell Police Station I assisted on live cyber fraud, online scam and social media abuse cases — supporting FIR documentation, digital evidence collection and case records, and helping victims through the reporting process. That work taught me to read suspicious digital activity carefully and to treat evidence as something that has to hold up later.

At Phoenix Cyber Secure I tested web applications, learned bug hunting fundamentals and identified straightforward security issues. Earlier, at Crypto Forensic Technology, I built the groundwork: ethical hacking fundamentals, reconnaissance, scanning and enumeration, including basic network scanning with Nmap.

Practical cybersecurity experience with an investigative mindset.
